# Brand Usage

StreamSignal supports Discord, Bluesky, and Mastodon integrations, but it is an independent tool.

Use platform names and logos only to identify supported integrations. Do not use platform logos in the StreamSignal logo, app icon, installer branding, product name, hero artwork, or marketing as a partnership signal.

Platform logo rules:

- Use official, bundled logo assets only.
- Do not recolor, stretch, crop, distort, animate, or combine platform logos with StreamSignal branding.
- Keep logos next to the relevant platform name or destination context.
- Keep the in-app Legal Notices text visible in Settings.
- Use a neutral fallback icon for unknown or unsupported platforms.

Required notice:

> StreamSignal is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Discord, Bluesky, Mastodon, or any Mastodon server operator. Platform names and logos are used only to identify supported integrations.
